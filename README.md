# Ink Blade

A one-thumb dash brawler for phones. Drag toward where you want to go and let go: while you're moving fast you destroy everything you touch.

- 10 brawlers, each with a trait, a Super, a Gadget and a Star Power, plus skins
- Survival, Maze, Daily Challenge and Boss Rush modes
- 18 enemy types and 3 bosses
- Endless progression: coins, brawler levels, daily quests, missions and player levels

## Install on your phone

Open the game's GitHub Pages link, then:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install app** when it appears, or open the ⋮ menu and choose **Add to Home screen**.

It opens full screen like a regular app and works offline after the first visit. Progress is saved on the device.

Multiplayer works app-to-app over the internet: team up in one shared arena or race in separate arenas. One player taps Create match and shares the 4-letter code.

## Android app

Every update to this repository builds a real Android app automatically.

1. On your Android phone, open **Releases** on this repository (or go straight to `https://github.com/ninjaivan2012-gif/ink-blade/releases/latest`).
2. Tap **ink-blade.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap **Install**.

New versions install over the old one and keep your progress.
